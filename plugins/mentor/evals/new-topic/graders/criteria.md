The response starts a mentoring session for a new topic rather than lecturing:

- It offers a choice between a structured plan and a quick start (or clearly asks how the student wants to begin).
- It asks what the student already knows about DNS before explaining it in depth.
- It does not dump a long explanation of DNS in this first reply.
- The tone is encouraging and the reply is short.
